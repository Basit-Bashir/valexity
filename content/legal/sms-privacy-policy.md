## Introduction

Valexity Technologies is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information in connection with our SMS/MMS messaging services, in compliance with applicable regulations including 10DLC messaging standards.

## Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name and phone number when you opt in to receive SMS communications from Valexity Technologies. In certain situations, device information or location data may also be collected as required for regulatory compliance.

## How We Obtain Consent

You provide explicit consent to receive SMS messages by opting in through our website forms, account interactions, or other approved consent methods. We do not send SMS messages without prior consent.

## How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to deliver service-related communications including customer support responses, account notifications, security alerts, authentication codes, and service updates related to Valexity Technologies services.

## Mobile Information Privacy

Valexity Technologies respects your privacy. Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent records, and opt-in information will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers such as messaging platforms or technical support vendors only when necessary to operate our services. These providers are required to safeguard your information and may not use it for their own marketing purposes.

## Messaging Terms

If you opt in to receive SMS messages from Valexity Technologies, you may receive messages related to:

- Customer support responses

- Two-factor authentication (2FA/MFA) passcodes

- Security alerts related to system activity

- Delivery notifications for hardware or service updates

Message frequency may vary depending on account activity and service requests. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying** STOP ** to any message. For assistance, reply **HELP** or contact us using the details below.
