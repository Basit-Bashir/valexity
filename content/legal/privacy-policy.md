At Valexity, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or reach out to us about our IT, telecom, or digital services — and how we use it.

## Information We Collect

### Information you give us

When you fill out a contact form, request a quote, or get in touch with us directly, you may share:

- Name and email address

- Phone number and company name

- Details about your current internet or telecom setup, such as your ISP name, account number, service address, and a copy of a recent bill (for telecom service inquiries)

- Security questions and answers (where account verification is needed)

- Any other information you choose to include in your message

### Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours automatically collects some technical data when you visit — things like your IP address, browser type, which pages you viewed, and how long you spent on them. On mobile, this also includes device type and operating system. We use this to understand how the site is performing and where we can improve it.

### Cookies and third-party tools

We use cookies to keep the site running and — with your consent — to power the following third-party services:

- **Google Analytics 4** — tracks traffic and usage patterns so we can improve the site. Only active after you accept analytics cookies. [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

- **Pagesense (Zoho)** — session recordings and heatmaps to help us understand how visitors use the site. Only active after analytics consent. [Zoho's Privacy Policy](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html)

- **Apollo.io** — identifies the company associated with a visitor's IP address for B2B outreach purposes. Under CCPA, this may qualify as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. Only active after you accept marketing cookies. [Apollo's Privacy Policy](https://www.apollo.io/privacy-policy)

- **Chatbase** — powers the support chat on our site. Only active after you accept functional cookies. [Chatbase's Privacy Policy](https://www.chatbase.co/privacy)

You can update or withdraw your cookie consent at any time by clicking **“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Info”** in our website footer.

Cookies may be session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (stored until they expire or you delete them). Essential cookies — the ones needed for basic site functionality — are always active and cannot be turned off.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Respond to your inquiries and service requests

- Deliver and manage the IT, telecom, and digital services you've engaged us for

- Send relevant updates about your account, services, or security matters

- Share information about other Valexity services you might find useful (you can opt out at any time)

- **Contact form submissions:** up to 3 years for business relationship management.

- **Consent records:** 3 years to document compliance.

Your information may be processed in locations outside your state or country where our service providers operate. By using our site, you acknowledge this.

## Your U.S. Privacy Rights

### California Residents — CCPA / CPRA

If you live in California, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

- **Right to Know** — request details about the personal information we've collected, why we collected it, and who we've shared it with.

- **Right to Delete** — ask us to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to limited exceptions.

- **Right to Correct** — ask us to fix inaccurate information.

- **Right to Opt Out of Sale or Sharing** — click **“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Info”** in our footer, or email [privacy@valexity.com](mailto:privacy@valexity.com). We also honor the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** browser signal and will automatically opt you out when detected.

- **Right to Limit Sensitive Data Use** — restrict how we use sensitive personal information to what's strictly needed to provide the service.

- **Right to Non-Discrimination** — we will never treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

### Categories of personal information we collect

- **Identifiers:** name, email, phone number, IP address, company name.

- **Commercial information:** services you've inquired about or purchased.

- **Network activity:** pages visited, time on site, browser type (via analytics).

- **Geolocation:** approximate location inferred from IP address.

- **Inferences:** drawn from the above to understand general preferences.

### Third parties we share personal information with

- **Analytics:** Google LLC (Google Analytics), Zoho Corporation (Pagesense).

- **Business outreach:** Apollo.io — this constitutes “sharing” under CCPA. Opt out via the footer link or GPC signal.

- **Communication:** EmailJS (contact forms), Chatbase (support chat).

### California Shine the Light — Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83

California residents may request a list of personal information we've shared with third parties for their own direct marketing purposes, once per calendar year. Email [privacy@valexity.com](mailto:privacy@valexity.com) with “Shine the Light Request” in the subject line.

### Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Texas Residents

Under state privacy laws (VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, TDPSA), residents of these states have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about them

- Correct inaccurate personal data

- Delete personal data they've provided

- Opt out of targeted advertising or the sale of personal data

- Obtain a portable copy of their personal data

To submit a request, contact us at [privacy@valexity.com](mailto:privacy@valexity.com). We'll respond within 45 days (or the timeframe required by applicable law). If we deny a request, we'll explain why and how to appeal.
